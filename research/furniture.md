## Caveats
- NO source gave an LP-per-cube count for KALLAX/EXPEDIT/EKET. Capacity is "unknown" for those; forum folklore of ~50 per cube is unsourced and not recorded. Suggest user-editable default.
- KALLAX 1x1, 1x2, 2x1 were NOT confirmed as currently sold (only dimensions.com 1x4, IKEA NZ partial list: 42x147, 77x77, 77x147, 112x147, 147x147, 182x182). IKEA NZ page showed 24 of 82 results; the IKEA US category page was blocked. 3x3 is not on the NZ partial list; dimensions.com only.
- Hex values are my approximations for UI rendering, not manufacturer values. The KALLAX "high-gloss white" and "gray" finishes come from dimensions.com only and may be regional/discontinued.
- BILLY (28 cm / 11 in deep) is too shallow for 12in LPs by inference; its finishes were not fetched (low-confidence, from general knowledge). BESTA has no verified specs (low). EKET: only the 35 cm cube is forum-confirmed to fit an LP.
- Retail pages for Magma LP-Bag 100/60, Zomo VS-Box 100/1 and 100/4, and Keep Them Spinning cubes flag items as discontinued or sold out; availability may differ by region.
- Several capacities conflict within one page (Zomo 100-120 vs 100-150; Vinyl Styl 75 vs 85-95); ranges are in notes, the lower-bound/primary figure is in capacityPerSlot/Total.
- Premium/audiophile items (Dual, Can-Am, Boltz, Siosi, Atocha, Department Chicago, LP Bin) come from a single 2010s-era Vinyl Factory article: prices/availability likely stale, all low confidence.
- Generic apple crate, record-store browser bin, wall ledge, Hama case, cardboard box: placeholders with unsourced or URL-only specs. Treat as user-customizable templates.
- Not found/not covered: Reloop-branded furniture, Wax Stacks current dimensions (waxstacks.com not fetched), Victorian credenzas, any verified LP flip-bin product specs.
