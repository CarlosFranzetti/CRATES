## Data caveats
- Hex codes are approximations from color names, not sampled from product photos. A designer should swap in real swatches.
- Capacity is as stated by the maker or retailer. Real-world fit varies: reviewers report fewer LPs with outer sleeves (Magma Riot, UDG StarterBag).
- Several pages contradict themselves (Zomo VS-Box 50, Odyssey CLP200P, Gator latch color, Crosley Platter-Pak). Each is noted in the item.
- Discontinued or sold-out items are flagged in notes (Magma LP-Bag 100 Trolley, LP-Bag 60 Profi, Zomo Technics bags, Reloop 80, Zomo VS-Box 100, UDG SoftBag 60).
- The "Generic Canvas Record Tote" entry is a placeholder preset, not a real product.
- Texture hints: ballistic nylon (Magma 40 II 1680D), tarpaulin (Magma Riot), carpet over plywood (Odyssey CLP), laminate ply with ball corners (Odyssey Flight Zone), coated MDF (Glorious, Zomo VS-Box), vinyl-wrap (Crosley, Gator).
